To use it every day: Open this file in Chrome or Edge on a computer, tablet or phone. On a phone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen, so it opens like an app with one tap. Text Smaller and Text Bigger at the top right change the print size.

Voice: Voice typing works best in Chrome or Edge. If the microphone button says it is not supported, type or dictate with your device keyboard instead — the big text box still works.

Record today: Tap "Click to Record" and speak, or type in the big text box. Pick how the day was, tap any topics and people, and set the day, the time of the note, and Wake and Bed times if you know them. Tap "Save this entry" — Wake and Bed are saved with the entry. While editing an entry you can delete it, and on a day with more than one note you can switch between them. When a day already has a note, the record button reads "Click to add to existing note" and a second button, "Add new note for the same day", appears beside it.

How was the day? In order — Good Day, Getting by, Tough day, Hard/Pain. If you tap Hard/Pain, the hospital faces pain scale pops up so you can tap a face from 0 (No Hurt) to 10 (Hurts Worst) for that entry.

Journal View: The month as an open notebook — two pages side by side, a large day number and month at the top of each page, the weekday, ruled lines, your entries, Wake, Bed and Sleep, and weather, with the week number at the foot of each page. The heading is simply the month itself, for example November 2026. Use Previous, Today and Next to move between months. Tap any day to record or edit it; a blank day shows only an "Add a note for this day" button.

Grid View: The same notes laid out like his paper page — date, weekday, note (with its mood dot at the start, and each note's time), Wake, Bed, Weather / Sun, one row for every day, with all entered months shown one after another separated by a horizontal month divider, at full browser width. Tap "Add a note for this day" on a blank day, or "Open this day" on a day that has a note, to record or edit it in full — or edit right here: tap a dot to change the mood, tap a note to edit its words, or tap a Wake or Bed time to change it.

Dashboard: A one-page summary — totals, mood counts, topics, who entries were about, wake/bed/sleep averages, weather averages, and every entry in that period. Choose This Month, Last Month, Pick a Month, All Time, or One Year to look at one period, or Compare Months to see two months side by side. Tap a topic to see its days. Print sits on the Dashboard title row, so it is always in reach.

Stories: Life stories, in your own words. Start the recording with a title — the first sentence you say or type becomes the title in the Index. Each finished sentence saves itself, so there is no need to press Save. Say "Paragraph" while recording to start a new paragraph, and "Stop recording" to stop. When the recording stops, a title box appears under the story — type a title there, or tap "Record Title" beside it to say the title instead, and the title saves itself when that recording stops. "Save this story" saves the story and any typed title together, or leave it blank. The title goes on the first line, with a blank line under it, and the words you recorded stay exactly as they were. "Clean Up Text" tidies the sentences: it drops a leading "And" from each one, capitalises the start of every sentence, and fixes a lone "i" to "I". Tap "View Index" in the Stories title bar to see all the titles, newest first; tap a title there to change it — the new title goes on the first line, with a blank line under it, and the words you recorded stay exactly as they were. Use the Copy, Export, View, Edit and Delete buttons beside a title to copy it to the clipboard, save it as a Word, text or PDF file, read it, change it, or remove it. "Export all Stories" in the Stories title bar saves every story into one Word, text or PDF file.

Weather: Toronto weather is fetched and saved automatically when you open a day, Journal View, or Grid View (needs internet). It shows in Journal View, Grid View, and Dashboard.

Backup: This log lives only in this browser on this device. Export a backup now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe. The backup includes notes, wake/bed times, saved weather, any topics or people you added, and stories. Older backups with notes only still import.

If you switch devices, import the backup file on the new device. Nothing is uploaded to the internet by this page.
